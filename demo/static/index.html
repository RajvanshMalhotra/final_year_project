    About this demo: what is real and what is emulated

    Three separate programs train a small language model together and average their updates every step over the network (PyTorch gloo). A crash really kills the program; the others notice it from missing heartbeats. The agent is the model trained in our simulator, unchanged, fed with these live measurements.
